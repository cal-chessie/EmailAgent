# Outreach copy rules — distilled for cohort-one

Sources: `cal-chessie/marketingskills` → skills/cold-email, copywriting,
copy-editing; `cal-chessie/agency-agents` → specialized/sales-outreach.
Read the full skills before writing any new campaign template.

## The non-negotiables (Cal, 2026-07-20)
1. **The email attracts. It never reveals the product.** The reveal happens
   on the booked call (playbook: COMH/RENEWABLY/PITCH_HALF_TIME_DOUBLE_PROFIT.md).
2. **The message is TIME** — hours lost to paperwork, handed back.
3. Sent as **AISolar in association with Renewably**. Never from renewably.ie.
4. Every send carries the opt-out line. Every send is Cal-approved.

## From the cold-email skill
- Write like a peer, not a vendor. Read it aloud; if it sounds like
  marketing copy, rewrite it.
- Ruthlessly short — every sentence must earn its place.
- "You/your" over "I/we". Lead with their world, never with who we are.
- One ask, low friction. Interest-based CTA; one-line reply must be enough.
- Personalisation must connect to the problem. If deleting the personalised
  line doesn't change the email, it isn't personalisation.
- Banned tells: "I hope this email finds you well", "I came across your
  profile", "leverage", "synergy", "best-in-class", clickbait subjects.
- Subjects: specific and human ("admin hours at {company}"), never
  "Quick question" / "Following up" boilerplate.

## The strongest anti-slop tool
Cal edits drafts by hand in the approval dashboard before sending. A real
human line about the actual company beats any template. Use the edit box.
